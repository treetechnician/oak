Assembly details

WP-1 · WP-2Window panels

  • One 4 × 8 ft foam sheet covers both cores plus the F-1 insulation wrap. Laminate with foam-safe adhesive.
  • Seal every penetration with foil tape or sealant on both faces. Fasten from inside only, so nothing can be removed from outdoors.
  • WP-2 hood points down or along the wall, never toward the neighbor's driveway. Expect frost and drip below it in deep cold.

F-1 · FE-501Intake train, panel to HRV

FE-501 sizing: a β ≈ 0.7 orifice in 6 in. duct (Cd ≈ 0.61) reads about 17 Pa at 70 cfm and 3 Pa at 30 cfm, well inside the SDP810-125's range, for roughly 9 Pa (0.035 in. w.c.) of permanent loss. Calibrate it in place against the Testo or a bag test.
